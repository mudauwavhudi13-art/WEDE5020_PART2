Welcome to Ubuntu Community Food Centre. We work to make sure food-insecure families in South Africa have access to nutritious food and community support.

Our goal is to bring people together and help build stronger and healthier communities.

About Us

Ubuntu Community Food Centre is a non-profit organisation that supports individuals and families experiencing food insecurity.

We believe that everyone deserves access to nutritious food, dignity and support within their community.

Our Mission

Our mission is to support food-insecure families by providing nutritious food, community support and opportunities for people to work together and make a positive difference.

Our Services

Food Parcels

We provide food parcels to families and individuals who need food assistance.

Prepared Meals

We provide prepared meals to support people in the local community.

Community Support

We offer community support programmes that help strengthen local families.

Volunteer Opportunities

Community members can volunteer their time and skills to support our work.

Donations

Donations of food, money and other resources help us continue supporting families.
